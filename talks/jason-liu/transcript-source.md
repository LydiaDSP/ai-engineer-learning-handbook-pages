## 摄影的例子怎样解释作品归属 / What photography reveals about ownership

[00:23:22 至 00:24:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y9JtCCZP2MQ&t=1402s)

摄影出现时，擅长如实再现的画家面临变化。Liu 用这个例子说明，一种工具能接手过去费力的环节，也会开出新的创作方式。

Early photography changed the landscape for painters who specialized in faithful representation. Liu uses it to show how a tool can take over laborious steps and open up a different form of work.

[00:24:19 至 00:25:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y9JtCCZP2MQ&t=1459s)

导演会决定机位、景别和观众该感到什么，也可能让团队一起实现。如今人人都能拍照，能留下多少照片与是否认真做了一张作品是两回事。

A director chooses the camera position, framing, and feeling, sometimes with a team implementing that vision. When everyone can take photos, the number saved says little about the care behind a particular image.

[00:25:13 至 00:27:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y9JtCCZP2MQ&t=1513s)

他把代码和画笔、黏土放在同一组例子里。工具帮人制作，创作者仍要认领最后做出的判断，并关心东西交到别人手上会怎样。

He puts code alongside paintbrushes and clay. Tools help make things, and the creator still owns the decisions that shape what other people receive.

## 把“这个界面不对劲”说清楚 / Learn to say what feels wrong about an interface

[00:27:42 至 00:27:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y9JtCCZP2MQ&t=1662s)

Liu 回忆，学艺术时他一度说不清留白的作用，便画草图探索。做应用时，他也会逐一琢磨为什么喜欢某个结果，又为什么觉得另一版不对劲。

Liu recalls drawing sketches in art school to understand negative space. He brings the same habit to building apps, asking what he likes about one result and what feels off about another.

[00:27:58 至 00:28:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y9JtCCZP2MQ&t=1678s)

在艺术学校，他的判断先于制作能力。反复说「再出挑一点」不能告诉他该改哪里。他把审美判断说得更具体，即能描述自己喜欢什么，也能预判别人喜欢什么。

In art school, his judgment outpaced his ability to execute. Repeating “make it pop” gave him little direction. He defines taste as describing what you like and predicting what others will like.

[00:28:42 至 00:29:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y9JtCCZP2MQ&t=1722s)

Liu 看到 Steve Ruiz 发帖分享一条查找应用里隐式状态机的提示词，便去查这个术语。他用生成器和判别器之间的差距作类比。模型的产出一旦接近人的评判上限，人就得继续学会辨别结果，才能指出改进方向。

Liu learned the term “implicit state machines” after seeing Steve Ruiz share a prompt for finding them in an app. He compares the situation to the gap between a generator and a discriminator. As model output approaches the limits of human judgment, people need to get better at telling what can still improve.

[00:29:51 至 00:31:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y9JtCCZP2MQ&t=1791s)

做得越多，他越常遇到新的盲点。网站不好看促使他学字体，应用反应生硬又促使他了解交互模式。词汇来自具体失败与参考作品的反复对照。

Making more applications kept exposing gaps. A weak website sent him to typography, and awkward applications sent him to interaction patterns. He built a more precise vocabulary from specific failures and work he could study.

[00:31:09 至 00:32:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y9JtCCZP2MQ&t=1869s)

Liu 建议做工具的人多用别人的工具，做叙事的人多看广告和电影。团队从 AI 省出的时间里留一点给探索，就有机会积累评判下一版作品的经验。

Liu suggests trying other people’s tools and looking at advertisements or films when making stories. If a team spends some of its saved time exploring, it can build the judgment it will bring to the next version.

## 回到以前没来得及学的东西 / Revisit skills and interests left on hold

[00:32:39 至 00:33:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y9JtCCZP2MQ&t=1959s)

在结尾，他请工程师想一想，从前为了进入软件行业暂停了哪些兴趣。AI 可以帮人以一个具体项目为入口，学陌生工具和术语。

Near the end, he asks engineers which interests they put aside when they chose software. AI can help them enter an unfamiliar field through a specific project and learn the tools and vocabulary along the way.

[00:33:28 至 00:36:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y9JtCCZP2MQ&t=2008s)

他以米开朗琪罗横跨雕塑、绘画、建筑和诗歌为例，说明掌握多门现代专业技能需要大量时间。把已有的工程判断带进一个陌生领域，仍能提出这个领域的人未必会想到的问题，再借 AI 试着做出来。

He contrasts Michelangelo’s work in sculpture, painting, architecture, and poetry with the time it takes to enter several modern specialties. An engineer can bring existing judgment into an unfamiliar field, ask a question an insider might overlook, and use AI to explore it.

[00:36:09 至 00:37:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y9JtCCZP2MQ&t=2169s)

他在学架子鼓时编写自己的音乐软件，把鼓连到 MIDI，用 AI 协助学习 Ableton。项目往前走一步，就能发现下一项真正需要了解的知识。

While learning drums, he wrote music software, connected his kit through MIDI, and used AI to help learn Ableton. Each step revealed the next thing he needed to understand.

[00:37:10 至 00:38:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y9JtCCZP2MQ&t=2230s)

他过去给新手的建议已经是做一个能用的天气或笔记应用。现在可以选更个人、更具体的麻烦来解决，也更容易把这种可能性展示给后来者。

His advice to beginners was already to make something useful, such as a weather or notes app. A learner can now choose a more personal problem and show later learners what they managed to build.
